# Document strategies

Load only the relevant section.

## Papers

For a focused question, locate the abstract, relevant method/result headings, limitations, and conclusion. Convert references only when citation tracing is required. For reproducibility questions, include formulas, algorithm steps, experimental setup, and the pages defining symbols or datasets.

## Datasheets

Search part numbers and terms such as `absolute maximum`, `electrical characteristics`, `pinout`, `register`, `timing`, `package`, and the requested parameter name. Include table headers, units, conditions, footnotes, and one or two context pages. Verify every critical min/typ/max value against the source page when extraction looks suspicious.

## Standards and specifications

Use bookmarks or contents to identify the clause. Include definitions and directly referenced clauses. Preserve clause numbers and distinguish physical PDF pages from printed labels.

## Manuals

Search error codes, feature names, commands, and prerequisites. Include warning/caution blocks and steps immediately before the target procedure.

## Scanned books or reports

Native search may fail. Convert the first 8–12 pages to find contents, then extend in small batches up to about 20 front-matter pages. Build a physical-page mapping before converting a target chapter. Do not OCR the entire work unless full coverage is explicitly required.

## Broad summaries

For short documents within the token budget, convert all pages. For long documents, sample the contents, executive summary or abstract, major section openings, findings, limitations, and conclusion, then disclose that the summary is selective unless full coverage was requested.
